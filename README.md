A world map summing up 22 November 2026 in one image: eight stories pinned
to their places — COP31 collapsing in Antalya, the Democrats' 258-seat House
win in Washington, two supertankers hit by mines near the Strait of Hormuz, a
Russian warship struck off Benghazi, drone attacks in Khartoum, the capture
of "El Chapo Isidro" in Sinaloa, a deadly storm in southern Brazil, and a
sixth weekend of protests in Paris.

The entry program is `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only). It draws
the whole poster with pure Python and writes `entry.png`.
